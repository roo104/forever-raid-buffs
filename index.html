World of Warcraft: Forever

Forever Raid Buffs

Drag classes into groups. Each group shows the buffs reaching it and the ones its members are missing.

Buff reach follows the Forever beta notes of October 15, 2026. Battle Shout, Trueshot Aura, Leader of the Pack, Moonkin Aura and the Strength of Earth, Grace of Air, Windfury and Flametongue totems are now raid-wide. Windfury and Flametongue don't stack, and Salvation no longer takes a blessing slot. Each player holds one blessing per paladin, so with two paladins every role gets its top two blessings. Paladin auras, Mana Spring, Mana Tide and Blood Pact still reach only the caster's party, marked PARTY on each chip. A pure melee group only lists melee buffs and a caster group only lists caster buffs. Hover a chip to see what it does.
